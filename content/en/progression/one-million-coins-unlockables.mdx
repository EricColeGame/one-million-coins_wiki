## What Counts as a One Million Coins Unlockable?

Every run in *One Million Coins* starts the same way: empty pockets, bare hands, and a vault stuffed with exactly 1,000,000 coins. What changes is what you can reach, carry, and cash in. The One Million Coins unlockables system is the engine that turns a slow hand-scooping chore into a roaring gold-collecting operation.

That matters because the game is short by design. Developer ShoXx built it as a focused experience that most players can finish in roughly two hours, which means every unlock you chase is a real, felt improvement rather than a grind for its own sake. Knowing what unlocks exist — and in what order they pay off — is the difference between a two-hour power fantasy and a four-hour slog.

This guide breaks down the One Million Coins unlockables by category: tools, carry gear, skill tree nodes, bunker infrastructure, and achievements.

## The Core Progression Loop

Before diving into individual unlocks, it helps to understand the loop that funds them. You collect gold from the pile, haul it to a deposit station, bank it into spendable funds, then spend those funds on the next upgrade. Repeat.

Nothing in the game is bought with abstract currency — you are literally carrying your own money to the counter. That design choice is why carry capacity matters so much early on. A bigger bag doesn't just save time; it multiplies the rate at which every other unlock becomes affordable.

| Stage | What You Unlock | What It Changes | Your Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Bare hands, no capacity upgrades | Slow, manual scooping | Learn the deposit loop |
| Early | Pouches and small bags | Larger loads per trip | Reduce round trips |
| Early-mid | First real tools | Faster collection per swing | Replace hand scooping |
| Mid | Deeper skill tree tiers | Movement, reach, speed, deposit rate | Pick a specialization |
| Mid-late | Bunker infrastructure | Automated hauling and lifting | Restore the facility |
| Late | High-powered collection tools | Huge waves of coins per action | Empty the vault |

The key takeaway: the loop never changes, but the numbers behind it do. Each unlock compresses the time between "I want that" and "I have it."

## Tools: The Headline One Million Coins Unlockables

Tools are the most visible upgrades in the game. You begin with nothing but your hands, then steadily replace manual labor with equipment that collects more coins per action and covers more ground.

The store page is deliberately vague about exact tool names, so treat any specific list you see online with caution. What is confirmed is the direction of travel: increasingly powerful tools that eventually pull in massive waves of coins and transform the vault into your own personal gold rush. Player experience suggests the biggest jumps in efficiency come from the first tool upgrade and from the first late-game tool, with a long middle stretch of incremental gains.

| Tool Tier | Collection Method | Relative Speed | Best Used For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hands | Manual scooping | Slowest | Learning the layout |
| Starter tool | Basic collection | Modest boost | Early banking |
| Mid-tier tools | Wider coverage | Solid gains | Clearing reachable areas |
| Late-game tools | Mass coin pulls | Dramatic | Emptying deep sections |

A practical tip: don't hoard for a single expensive tool if a cheaper upgrade is one deposit away. In incremental games, small compounding gains usually beat saving for a big spike, especially in a title this short.

## Carry Gear: Pouches, Backpacks, and Capacity

Capacity is the quiet MVP of the One Million Coins unlockables list. Collection speed only matters if you can carry what you collect, and deposit rate only matters if you're actually depositing.

Bags and pouches expand how much gold you can haul per trip, and the skill tree adds further capacity on top of that. Community reports consistently flag capacity as one of the most underrated early investments, because a bigger load improves every single trip you take for the rest of the run.

| Carry Unlock | Source | Effect | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pouches | In-game purchase | Small capacity boost | High — buy early |
| Backpacks | In-game purchase | Larger capacity boost | High |
| Capacity nodes | Skill tree | Stackable capacity gains | Medium-high |
| Infrastructure hauling | Bunker restoration | Reduces manual carrying | Medium |

If you're wondering where to spend your first meaningful chunk of banked gold, capacity is rarely the wrong answer.

## The 250+ Node Skill Tree Across Seven Paths

The skill tree is the deepest progression system in *One Million Coins*. It spans more than 250 nodes and seven upgrade paths, and it's where your run starts to look different from someone else's.

The store description specifically calls out improvements to movement, reach, collection speed, tool strength, capacity, and deposit rate — six distinct levers you can pull. Because the tree is that large and the run is that short, you will not fill it out. You will shape it.

| Upgrade Path | What It Improves | Who Should Prioritize It |
|---|---|---|
| Movement | How fast you traverse the vault | Players who feel slow between the pile and the deposit station |
| Reach | How far you can collect from | Players clearing awkward, deep sections |
| Collection speed | Coins gathered per action | Anyone still scooping by hand |
| Tool strength | Power of equipped tools | Players investing in equipment |
| Capacity | Load size per trip | Nearly everyone, early on |
| Deposit rate | How fast banked gold converts | Players with fast collection but slow banking |

**Practical advice:** pick two paths and commit. Splitting a limited budget across all seven paths leaves you mediocre at everything, which feels worse than being excellent at two things.

## Infrastructure Unlocks: Restoring the Bunker

Not every One Million Coins unlockable is something you wear or swing. As you dig deeper into the underground vault, you can restore the bunker's own infrastructure — deposit stations, cargo lifts, conveyors, and other facility systems.

These unlocks are the closest thing the game has to automation. A restored conveyor or cargo lift moves coins on your behalf, which effectively converts a manual task into a passive one. That's a huge deal in a two-hour game, because passive income frees you to spend your attention on the next upgrade instead of the last one.

| Infrastructure | Function | Best Time to Restore |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit stations | Convert collected gold into spendable funds | Immediately when available |
| Cargo lifts | Move loads vertically through the bunker | After expanding carry capacity |
| Conveyors | Automate coin transport | Once collection outpaces hauling |
| Bunker systems | Support deeper excavation | When progress stalls |

The general rule: restore infrastructure whenever manual hauling has become the bottleneck. If you're spending more time walking than collecting, the answer isn't a faster tool — it's a machine that walks for you.

## Achievements as Unlockables

*One Million Coins* ships with 16 Steam achievements, which function as a meta layer of unlockables. Four are visible on the store page: **Pocket Change**, **An Honest Day's Work**, **Change Management**, and **A Small Fortune**. The remaining twelve are hidden until discovered.

| Achievement | Suggested Theme | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pocket Change | First small haul | Likely an early-game milestone |
| An Honest Day's Work | Completing a work cycle | Possibly tied to banking gold |
| Change Management | Managing currency or upgrades | Suggests a spending or upgrade threshold |
| A Small Fortune | Accumulating a large sum | Mid-to-late progression marker |

Treat the themes column as inference, not fact — the store page doesn't publish requirement text. If you're an achievement hunter, the good news is that the game supports Steam Cloud and Steam Leaderboards, so progress and comparisons carry across sessions.

## Tips for Unlocking Everything Faster

- **Bank constantly.** Funds sitting in your pockets aren't buying upgrades.
- **Buy capacity before power.** It compounds on every trip.
- **Specialize your skill tree.** Two focused paths beat seven shallow ones.
- **Restore infrastructure when hauling becomes the bottleneck**, not before.
- **Play at your own pace.** The game supports it by design — no quick-time events, no forced timers.
- **Save anytime.** If you're experimenting with a build, you can always step back.
- **Don't chase the million.** Chasing the *next unlock* is what actually empties the vault.

One final note on scope: the game is single-player and supports Family Sharing, so there's no competitive pressure to rush your One Million Coins unlockables. The leaderboards exist, but the intended experience is cozy, not cutthroat.

## FAQ

**How many One Million Coins unlockables are there?**
There's no official count, but the confirmed systems include a growing tool set, pouches and backpacks, a 250+ node skill tree across seven upgrade paths, restored bunker infrastructure such as deposit stations, cargo lifts, and conveyors, plus 16 Steam achievements.

**How long does it take to unlock everything?**
The developer states most players can work through the vault in around two hours. Fully completing the skill tree is unlikely in that window, which means build choices genuinely matter.

**Do One Million Coins unlockables carry over between sessions?**
The game supports Steam Cloud, so progress is preserved across sessions and devices where Cloud is available.

**Is there a fastest order for unlocking things?**
Community reports suggest the reliable order is capacity first, then collection speed, then infrastructure automation, then late-game tools. Prioritize whichever of those is currently your biggest bottleneck.

**Does the game have a competitive mode?**
No. It's a single-player, first-person incremental game with leaderboards and achievements layered on top for players who want them.
